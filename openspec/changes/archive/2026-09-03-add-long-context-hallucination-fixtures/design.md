## Context

The existing reliability runner executes small, version-controlled cases through the real DSH driver. Its nine core fixtures are good smoke coverage, but they do not expose degradation when a work grows to tens of thousands of Chinese characters. This change prepares deterministic synthetic long-form materials and cases; execution remains staged so a 162-call run is not required in one session.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Provide approximately 10k, 30k, and 50k-character Chinese synthetic materials.
- Place auditable facts at known positions across chapters, with distractors, similar entities, timeline changes, and version history.
- Keep an oracle outside the material text so expected answers can be checked without sending the oracle to the model.
- Represent 12, 18, and 24 questions per tier and three planned trials per question.
- Make each tier runnable independently with deterministic hashes and safe evidence handling.

**Non-Goals:**

- Do not modify the production driver or user-facing writing features.
- Do not improve automatic natural-language screening in this change.
- Do not require all 162 API calls to run during implementation.
- Do not use real user documents or claim that synthetic results prove general model reliability.

## Decisions

- **Deterministic generator over hand-authored huge files.** A seeded generator keeps the three materials reproducible and makes regeneration auditable; checked-in outputs remain available to the existing JSON fixture loader.
- **Checked-in material plus separate oracle metadata.** The material is what the model receives. The oracle records expected facts, locations, wrong conclusions, and query categories without being injected into the driver.
- **Character-count tiers with tolerance.** Targets are approximately 10k, 30k, and 50k Chinese characters, with recorded actual counts and hashes. Character counts are more transparent for this Chinese corpus than pretending they are exact token budgets.
- **One case per question and explicit trial metadata.** Existing runner semantics remain intact. A case has a stable question identity and a trial plan of three; a later runner change can execute or aggregate trials without changing the factual oracle.
- **Staged execution documentation.** The first operational run is the 10k tier, followed by 30k and 50k. This isolates context-limit or protocol failures and controls API cost.
- **Coherent narrative comparison tier.** The seeded template generator has unknown-direction bias: its filler noise may inflate entity confusion, while its verbatim-unique anchor sentences may make retrieval unrealistically easy. To bracket the real-world risk, we add one hand-authored, approximately 10k-character coherent narrative tier. Its prose has scene continuity, stable characters, and causal flow; its anchors are expressed in natural (not verbatim-marked) sentences, so recall depends on semantic understanding rather than surface uniqueness. Its query matrix mirrors the 10k template tier (12 queries, seven risk categories, trial_count=3) so the two tiers are directly comparable. The coherent tier is marked `handwritten`, is not regenerated by the seeded generator, and skips the regeneration-equality check while keeping all other validation.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Template noise bias is unknown in direction and magnitude] → Keep it as an upper-bound adversarial probe; add the coherent narrative tier as a real-proximity comparison, and report both without conflating them.

- [Synthetic prose may not represent every real writing style] → Use it to measure controlled retrieval and contradiction risks, then label results as synthetic evidence.
- [Character count does not equal provider token count] → Record actual character counts and estimated token counts; treat provider limits as an execution concern.
- [Large checked-in fixtures increase repository size] → Keep prose repetitive but semantically varied, and store only the three required tiers plus generator metadata.
- [Existing scorer may classify correct quoted answers as NEEDS_REVIEW] → Preserve raw responses and report scorer uncertainty separately; defer scorer changes to a later change.
- [One long replay may exceed driver/provider limits] → Run tiers independently and make protocol/runtime failures explicit in evidence.
